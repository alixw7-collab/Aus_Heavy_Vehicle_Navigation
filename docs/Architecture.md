\# Heavy Vehicle Navigation



\## Vision



Build the world's best heavy vehicle navigation platform by combining official road access data with real operational knowledge from professional drivers.



\## Objectives



\- Validate legal routes

\- Reduce compliance risk

\- Support B-Doubles, A-Doubles, Road Trains and PBS vehicles

\- Integrate NHVR network data

\- Capture depot and customer access knowledge

\- Eventually provide turn-by-turn navigation



\## Principles



\- Official data first

\- Driver knowledge second

\- Every rule must have a source

\- Never guess access
